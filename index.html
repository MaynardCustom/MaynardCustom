About

A one-man shop with an obsession for detail.

Maynard Custom started with a simple idea: building PCs shouldn't be a transaction, it should be a craft. I'm a builder who takes the time to understand how you'll use your machine — then obsesses over the parts, airflow, and cable routing until it's exactly right.

From quiet office boxes to showcase water-cooled rigs, every build gets the same treatment: quality components, clean cable management, and thorough stress testing before it ships or is handed over in person.

  • Transparent, itemized quotes — no hidden fees
  • Quality parts sourced from trusted retailers
  • Full stress tests & burn-in before delivery
  • Local pickup or delivery available
  • Post-build support, upgrades & repairs
