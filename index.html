The Walnut Rig

A quiet, mesh-and-wood build around a used 3080 FE — chosen for its blower-adjacent shroud and the way it plays with the case's warm front panel. Built for a client who wanted something that didn't look like a gaming PC in daylight, and lit up like one at night.

Case
Corsair 4000D Frame, wood front
CPU

GPU

Memory
32GB DDR4 3200MHz
Storage
2TB M.2 SSD (used) + 1TB SATA SSD
PSU

CPU Cooler
